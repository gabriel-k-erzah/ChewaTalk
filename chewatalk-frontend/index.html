Learn Nyanja (Chichewa) one word at a time

ChewaTalk is a free way to learn Nyanja, also called Chichewa or Chinyanja: the language of Lusaka and much of Zambia and Malawi. Short flashcard decks, quick quizzes and a matching game take you from your first greetings to real conversations, coached by Nkwazi the eagle and Goggles the dog.

Five levels

  • Level 1, First Words: greetings, introductions, basic words, numbers
  • Level 2, Everyday Vocabulary: family, food and drinks, colours, animals, common actions
  • Level 3, Building Sentences: directions, shopping, questions, daily activities
  • Level 4, Conversations: social chat, feelings, time and plans, health and emergencies
  • Level 5, Real-World Fluency: longer conversations, stories and opinions

Survival kit

Phrases for getting by: travel, the market, food words, days of the week, and how to ask someone to slow down.

Where the translations come from

Every translation comes from a named source, never machine translation: the Peace Corps/Zambia 1995 Nyanja lessons and the Wikitravel Nyanja phrasebook. Phrases with no checked translation yet are clearly marked as waiting.
